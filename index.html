ABOUT THE EVENT

Where everyday things get smarter

iEXPO V6.0 is BCAS Campus's annual IoT Exhibition — a full day where students showcase working projects that connect sensors, devices, the cloud and apps into real solutions.

Whether you are a student, parent, teacher, industry professional or simply curious about technology, you are warmly invited to walk through, ask questions and be inspired.
